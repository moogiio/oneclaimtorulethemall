OneClaimToRuleThemAll

Click together your AD FS claim rules

Build claim rules by clicking, reuse them across applications, try a sign-in, and give your AD FS admin one PowerShell script that shows the changes before it applies them.

  • A guided setup for new applications, with templates for AWS, ServiceNow, Google Workspace, Zoom, Slack and more. Or give it the app's metadata file, and it picks the claims the app asks for
  • Every rule in plain words, a linter with one-click fixes, and Try it to see what a test user gets. Sign-in fails? Paste the AD FS error and get it explained
  • Import a whole AD FS farm with a read-only script. Export a script that previews changes, or steps to click through
There are no accounts. Your workspace gets a long secret link, and anyone with the link can view and edit it. Bookmark it and only share it with people you trust. Lost links cannot be recovered.

Rather keep your claim rules on your own machine? The app is open source: get it from github.com/moogiio/oneclaimtorulethemall and run it locally with Node.js.
